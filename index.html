How to Play

  • Match generation to demand — too little or too much pushes frequency away from 60 Hz.
  • Sell electricity automatically every tick. Build more plants in the Market tab to sell more.
  • Claim Grid Contracts when they appear for quick cash bonuses.
  • Run renewables (solar, wind, hydro, geothermal) to build up Carbon Credits you can sell anytime.
  • Keep frequency steady for a while to earn a passive Stability Bonus.
  • Coal and gas plants are cheap but emit CO2, which costs you a carbon tax — balance cost vs. cleanliness.
  • If a plant trips offline, repair it from its card to bring it back online.
